# Thiru Annamalai Food Products — UI/UX Audit Framework

**Project Type:** E-commerce Website (Traditional Indian Food Products)  
**Tech Stack:** React, TanStack Router, Tailwind CSS, Radix UI  
**Target Audience:** Health-conscious consumers, Tamil diaspora, traditional food enthusiasts  
**Primary Goal:** Convert visitors into customers through trust, authenticity, and seamless shopping experience

---

## Framework Overview

This audit framework is specifically designed for the Thiru Annamalai food products e-commerce website. It focuses on:

- **E-commerce conversion optimization**
- **Cultural authenticity and trust signals**
- **Product presentation and discoverability**
- **Mobile-first shopping experience**
- **Food safety and quality communication**
- **Traditional brand storytelling**

# PROMPT 1 — E-COMMERCE UI/UX AUDIT

## Purpose

This stage focuses on identifying:
- **E-commerce conversion barriers** — friction in the purchase journey
- **Product presentation weaknesses** — how products are showcased
- **Trust signal gaps** — missing credibility indicators
- **Mobile shopping friction** — mobile-specific usability issues
- **Cart and checkout problems** — abandonment risk factors
- **Food product-specific concerns** — ingredients, certifications, freshness
- **Cultural authenticity issues** — Tamil heritage representation
- **Visual hierarchy problems** — what draws attention vs. what's ignored

The purpose is to create a detailed understanding of:
- What prevents visitors from becoming customers
- What reduces trust in product quality
- What creates confusion in the shopping journey
- What fails to communicate brand authenticity
- What causes cart abandonment

This stage builds the foundational analysis required before any redesign decisions are made.
